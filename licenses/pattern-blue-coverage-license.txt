# NOTICE

The **Case Hardened coverage dataset** in `data/case-hardened.json` is a
normalised derivative of the Blue Gem pattern dataset published under MIT
license as part of the CSFloat Market Checker browser extension
(<https://github.com/csfloat/extension>). The raw upstream schema was
restructured into the camel-case nested form documented in this repository.

Upstream copyright notice, preserved per the terms of its MIT license:

    MIT License
    Copyright (c) 2017 Stepan Fedorko-Bartos

Pattern IDs and per-seed colour statistics are objective facts about CS2
item rendering and are not subject to copyright in their own right. The
compilation and presentation in this repository are released under the MIT
license — see LICENSE.

Doppler phase paintIndex mappings (`data/doppler-phases.json`) are public
CS2 game data widely documented across the trading community. They are
included here for convenience.

Weapon / paint name mappings (`data/weapons.json`) are public CS2 game
data. They are included as a small convenience lookup — this repository
does not attempt to be a complete CS2 item database.
